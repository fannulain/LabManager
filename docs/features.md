# LabManager Features

LabManager (Educational Materials and Laboratory Manager) is an information system designed to organize and optimize the educational process. Below is a breakdown of its core features based on the project specifications:

## 1. Material Structuring
Organizes educational resources and assignments into a clear, structured, and accessible format. This ensures that students and educators can quickly navigate and find necessary course materials, lectures, and lab instructions.

## 2. Deadline Tracking
Provides a built-in system to track and display upcoming deadlines for laboratory work and other academic assignments. This feature helps students manage their workload effectively and ensures that important dates are never missed.

## 3. Material Archive
Maintains a historical archive of educational materials from previous semesters. This allows educators to easily reference, reuse, or adapt past resources for future courses, while keeping the current semester's workspace uncluttered.
